Do I need a gym to join a program?
Absolutely not. Every program is built around the equipment you actually have access to — whether that's a commercial gym, a home setup with a few dumbbells, or even bodyweight only. During the onboarding, Jordan assesses your setup and builds your plan accordingly. Equipment is never the barrier to results.
How much time do I need to commit per week?
Most programs are designed for 3–5 training sessions per week, each lasting 45–75 minutes. You'll also have a brief weekly check-in call (30 min). The total is typically 4–7 hours per week — all of which is planned around your schedule during onboarding, not ours.
What if I have an injury or health condition?
Injuries and health considerations are handled during our initial assessment. Jordan is certified in corrective exercise and works with clients managing everything from bad knees to post-surgery recovery. We never work around your health — we build the program with it in mind. If a medical clearance is needed, we'll let you know up front.
How does online coaching actually work?
After onboarding, you receive your full program through our coaching app. You log workouts, track food, and message Jordan directly. Each week, you do a short check-in (video or written), and Jordan reviews your data, updates the plan, and sends personalized feedback. Form videos can be submitted any time for review. It's more support than most in-person trainers provide.
What's the refund/guarantee policy?
If you complete the 12-week program — meaning you follow the training, nutrition, and check-in protocols — and don't experience meaningful results, we'll continue coaching you at no charge until you do. We've never had to honor this because the method works. But the guarantee exists because we stand fully behind what we deliver.
Can I do nutrition-only coaching without a training program?
Yes — the Nutrition Mastery program is designed exactly for this. It's ideal for people who already have a training routine they love but need to fix their relationship with food, understand macros, and build sustainable eating habits. Jordan can also combine a light training plan with it if desired.
How many clients do you take on at once?
Jordan limits active 1-on-1 clients to 20 at any time to ensure everyone gets genuine, personalized attention — not a generic program with an automated check-in. When we're full, we maintain a waitlist. Spots typically open each month as clients complete their programs. Book a call to find out current availability.
